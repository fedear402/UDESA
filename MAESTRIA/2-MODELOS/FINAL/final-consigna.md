Consider a small open economy with only a final good production (there is only one good) and where the only foreign asset available is a foreign bond.  There is a country risk rule for determining the interest rate based on how much the country lends or borrows from the rest of the world.

Households own capital, deposits in the domestic banking system, and foreign bonds (which could be positive or negative).  They supply labor to the local firms and rent out their capital.  Households have a cash in advance constraint, using only money brought over from the previous period, the cash in advance constraint includes deposits in the bank.

Banks take deposits from the households and lend to the firms money for working capital that is used to cover their wage bill. They pay back their loans at the end of the period.  Banks are competitive.

The central bank follows a Taylor rule with a=.5 and b=.5 using current output and current inflation in the Taylor rule.

Firms borrow working capital from the bank to cover their wage bill and pay interest to the banks for using the capital.  They rent physical capital from the households.  Firms are competitive.
