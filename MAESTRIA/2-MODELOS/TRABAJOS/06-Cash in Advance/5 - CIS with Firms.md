Now, firms have a cash in advance restriction on the wages they have to pay

Firms maximize:
$$
\pi^{j}_{t} = y^{j}_{t} -w_{t}h^{j}_{t} - r_{t}k_{t}^{j} 
$$
$$
\text{sa:} \qquad y_{t}^{j} = (k_{t}^{j} )^{\theta} ()
$$

Money held by firm $j$ at time $t$ is $m^{j}_{t}$.
Total money
$$
M^{J}_{t} = \int _{0}^{1} m^{j}_{t} \, dj 
$$

Money held by consumer $i$ at time $t$ is $m^{i}_{t}$.
$$
M_{t}^{I} = \int _{0}^{1} m^{i}_{t} \, di 
$$
Aggregate money supply in the economy:
$$
M_{t}= M_{t}^{J} + M_{t}^{I}
$$
